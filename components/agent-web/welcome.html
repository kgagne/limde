This window is for your web agent

Pick the AI assistant you use on the web and sign in the way it asks. This window remembers it: from now on it opens straight on your agent, stays signed in, and keeps its place at the left of the panel.

Claudeclaude.ai ChatGPTchatgpt.com Geminigemini.google.com Copilotcopilot.microsoft.com Le Chatchat.mistral.ai Perplexityperplexity.ai

Good to know

  • Links to other sites open in your main Firefox, so this window stays on your agent.
  • It reopens by itself if it closes. To close it until your next login, run agent-web stop; to turn it off, see components/agent-web/README.md.
  • Using a different agent, or a page not listed here? Run agent-web set-home https://…, or agent-web reset to choose again.
